## Overview

The Institute of Sustainability, Governance and Management needed more than a public-facing institutional website. The organisation required a coordinated digital system capable of presenting professional Programs, receiving applications, collecting processing fees, supporting admissions decisions and managing the certification journey from one platform.

Kemma Technologies designed and developed an end-to-end institutional platform connecting the public website, applicant experience and administrative operations.

## The Challenge

Professional training and certification Programs involve multiple operational stages: Program discovery, applications, document submission, fee payment, admissions decisions, admission documentation, cohort coordination, certification and public certificate verification.

Handling these processes through disconnected forms, spreadsheets, email threads and manual files creates delays, inconsistent records and unnecessary administrative work. The platform needed to make the process clear for applicants while giving staff a dependable internal system.

## Our Approach

We built a unified platform on Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase and Paystack covering structured Program discovery, online applications, secure applicant accounts, document uploads, payment processing, applicant dashboards, role-based staff administration, admission-letter generation, certificate management and public certificate verification.

## Outcomes

- Live institutional platform at [pisgm.org](https://pisgm.org)
- Public Program catalogue with online applications and Paystack fee collection
- Applicant accounts with status tracking and document workflows
- Role-based administrative workflows for review, decisions and certification
- Generated admission documents and public certificate verification
